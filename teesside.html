Staff Testimonial

"In my role as a Student Journey Advisor, I actively gather and represent the student voice through a range of engagement methods, including student voice meetings, focus groups, and surveys. I support students' transition into university life by coordinating transition events and delivering induction and orientation activities. These initiatives are designed to foster a sense of belonging among new students and ensure they feel welcomed and supported. Additionally, I lead on the development of students as partners projects, embedding the student voice into institutional practices and enhancing the overall student experience. Participation in the regional meeting has provided valuable opportunities for in-depth discussions around the work being carried out across the North East. It has enabled the sharing of best practices and the generation of new ideas to further enhance our approach."

- Emma Forster, Student Journey Advisor
